# pokemon-model

A Pokémon Champions VGC (Regulation M-C) model and advisor: WP models, an endgame solver, a belief
over hidden spreads, and the web app that serves them.

## Living docs

These are kept current. Add to them when something new is measured or confirmed:

@docs/principles.md
@docs/format-facts.md

## Plan documents are snapshots

`docs/PLAN*.md` and `docs/phase*-findings.md` record what was being built, and what was found, at
the time they were written. The newest `PLAN-vN.md` is the current plan and is revised as work
goes; once superseded, a plan is an archive. Don't rewrite archives, or the principles lists the
plans carry, to reflect later decisions; code cites their step numbers. A standing fact or
principle goes in the living docs above, not in a plan.
